# Image Sourcing — Radial Velocity

Guidance for adding illustrative images to this repo. Two tracks: real
archival imagery first, AI-generation as a fallback. Every image needs
a caption naming the real mission/instrument depicted — AI-generated
images must say so explicitly and must never name the generating tool.

## Real sources to check first

- ESO press-image library — HARPS at La Silla, ESPRESSO at VLT
  (CC-BY-4.0 images available) — <https://www.eso.org/public/images/>
- Keck Observatory image gallery (HIRES) —
  <https://www.keckobservatory.org/press-room/>

**hero:** search "HARPS spectrograph La Silla ESO press photo" or a
Doppler-wobble diagram from ESO's 51 Pegasi b press materials.

**instrument:** a real HARPS or ESPRESSO instrument/telescope photo
from eso.org (check each image's specific CC-BY-4.0 license terms
before use).

## AI-generation fallback prompts

- **hero:** "Artist's concept of a star wobbling slightly around a
  common center of mass with an orbiting Jupiter-like planet, motion
  trail arcs, deep space"
- **3d:** "Close-up render of stellar absorption-line spectrum
  shifting red and blue, spectrograph visualization style"
- **instrument:** "Photorealistic illustration of the HARPS
  spectrograph dome at La Silla Observatory at night under stars"

## Citation rule

- AI-generated: caption states "AI-generated illustration/concept —
  not a real photograph," names the real mission/instrument, never
  names the generating tool.
- Real archival photo: cite the actual observatory/mission directly —
  no AI-credit line needed.
